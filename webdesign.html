The Rocinante process

From first call to ongoing care

We keep the process simple, clear, and fast so you always know what is happening and what comes next.

  1. 01

    Discovery Call

    We start with a short call to learn about your business, goals, and what you want your website to do. No sales pitch, just a straightforward conversation to see if it is a good fit.

  2. 02

    Transparent Proposal

    After the call, you receive a clear proposal outlining exactly what is included, how the site will be built, and your monthly rate. No surprise fees, no jargon.

  3. 03

    Design Approval

    We create an initial design direction for your site. You review layout, structure, and overall look before any build work moves forward, so everything is aligned from the start.

  4. 04

    Your Site Comes to Life

    Once approved, we design and hand-code your website. Everything is custom built, fast loading, and responsive across devices. No templates or WordPress.

  5. 05

    Feedback Loop

    You preview the site and share feedback. We refine design, copy, and layout until everything feels right for your business and service area.

  6. 06

    Launch Day

    We handle final setup and launch. Your site goes live fast, stable, and ready for customers to call or request service.
