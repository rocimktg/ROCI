Common Questions About Home Service Website Design

Do home service contractors really need a website?

Yes — most homeowners search before they call, even when a neighbor referred them. A Google search, a website, and a Business Profile are how homeowners decide if you're legit. Without a website, you're invisible in search, and word of mouth alone has a ceiling. A fast, professional site makes every other marketing effort — Google, Nextdoor, yard signs, referrals — work harder.

What should a home service contractor website include?

At minimum: your services, your service area, a way to request an estimate, your phone number (tap-to-call on mobile), and real proof — photos of your work and reviews from real customers. Beyond that, service-specific pages, before-and-after galleries, a Google Business Profile that matches your site, and schema markup help search engines understand your business and show it to the right people.

Do you use WordPress?

No. We hand-code every site from scratch — no WordPress, no templates, no page builders. That means faster load times (often under a second), nothing to break from plugin updates, and a site that doesn't slow down or get compromised because of a theme vulnerability. It also means every element is built specifically for your business, not adapted from someone else's layout.

Can you use my existing photos and reviews?

Yes. Send us your photos and we'll work with them. If you don't have many, we'll help you figure out what shots will make the biggest difference and how to get them easily — often with just a phone. For reviews, we'll pull from your Google Business Profile and feature the strongest ones on your site.

How fast can the site launch?

Most sites launch within 7 to 10 business days after kickoff — once we have the content we need. The biggest delay is almost always content: photos, your service list, your service area, and a few sentences about your business. We'll walk you through exactly what we need so it's fast and simple on your end.

Will this help me get more calls?

A well-built site won't conjure calls from thin air — but it will convert more of the traffic you already get, support every referral that searches you online to verify you're real, and give search engines a stronger signal about what you do and where you work. Combined with the Google Business Profile setup and audit included in both plans, most clients see a meaningful uptick in inbound calls within the first few months.
