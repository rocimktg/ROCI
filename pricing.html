What's Included — Explained

What Every Feature Does

01

Mobile-First Design

Most customers searching for a contractor are on their phone. We design mobile first so your site loads fast, looks sharp, and drives action on any screen — whether they're at the kitchen table or in their driveway.

02

Tap-to-Call Buttons

A persistent tap-to-call button makes it effortless for mobile visitors to reach you without hunting for your number. One tap — that's all it takes to turn a search into a lead.

03

Website Structure

Your site is organized around what matters most: who you are, what you do, where you work, and how to get a quote. Lite gets 1 page covering all the essentials; Growth and Pro add service or city pages on top of that.

04

Local Search Structure

Schema markup tells Google exactly what your business does and where. It helps your pages appear for the right local searches and improves your shot at the local map pack — where most service calls actually start.

05

Review Request Page

A clean page at your domain (yourbusiness.com/feedback) lets customers leave a Google review with one tap right after a completed job. Send the link by text — no searching, no confusion, no missed opportunities.

06

Website Updates

Growth and Pro include unlimited edits — send a text or email and we handle it fast. No support tickets, no delays — direct access to the person who built your site. Lite includes an annual content refresh plus operational edits like phone number changes, photo swaps, and service updates.

07

Google Business Profile Support

Your Google Business Profile is often the first impression a homeowner gets before they ever visit your website. We keep it complete, accurate, and active — so it works as hard as your site to drive calls and quote requests.

08

Monthly Visibility Work

Search is changing, but your site still needs to stay clear, current, and useful. Each month, we make focused improvements to your services, locations, FAQs, project proof, reviews, or search structure. See pricing table for details.

09

Call Tracking

A tracked phone number on your site logs every incoming call so you can see exactly how many leads your website generates. Available on Growth and Pro — know your numbers, prove your ROI.

10

Missed-Call Textback

When someone calls and you don't pick up, they automatically receive a text within minutes so you don't lose the lead. On Growth and Pro — your business keeps responding even when you're on the job.

11

Google Search Campaigns

Google Search campaigns put your business at the top of results when homeowners in your area are actively looking for what you do. Pro plan includes up to 3 focused campaigns with setup, targeting, and ongoing optimization.

12

LSA Support

Local Services Ads (Google Guaranteed) show your business above organic results and Search Ads. Pro plan includes setup and ongoing maintenance where available — ad spend goes directly to Google.
