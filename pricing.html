What's Included — Explained

What Every Feature Does

01

Mobile-First Design

Most customers searching for a contractor are on their phone. We design mobile first so your site loads fast, looks sharp, and drives action on any screen — whether they're at the kitchen table or in their driveway. Every page includes a persistent tap-to-call button so visitors can reach you in one tap, without hunting for your number.

02

Website Structure

Your site is organized around what matters most: who you are, what you do, where you work, and how to get a quote. Monthly gets up to 5 pages covering all the essentials. Lump Sum includes 5 core pages plus up to 10 service or service area pages.

03

Local Search Structure

Schema markup tells Google exactly what your business does and where. It helps your pages appear for the right local searches and improves your shot at the local map pack — where most service calls actually start.

04

Review Request Page

A clean page at your domain (yourbusiness.com/feedback) lets customers leave a Google review with one tap right after a completed job. Send the link by text — no searching, no confusion, no missed opportunities.

05

Brand-Matched Design

We don't drop your logo into a template. We pull your real colors, your photos, and the look of your trucks and signage so the site feels like your company the second a homeowner lands on it. That recognition — "this looks like my business" — is what builds trust before they ever call.

06

Call Tracking Number

A dedicated tracking number on your site shows you exactly how many calls your website is driving — so you're never guessing whether it's working. Calls forward straight to your real phone; you just get the visibility.

07

Missed-Call Text-Back

When you're on a roof or running a chainsaw, you can't always answer. The second a call goes unanswered, your site automatically texts that customer back — "Sorry we missed you, how can we help?" — so the lead stays warm instead of dialing your competitor. Every missed call becomes a conversation instead of a lost job.

08

Lead Notifications

The moment someone calls or submits a form, you get an instant alert with their info — so you can follow up while you're still top of mind. No checking voicemail hours later when the job's already gone.

09

Monthly Performance Summary

Once a month, you get a plain-English recap of what your site actually did: calls driven, missed calls recovered by text, and form fills. No dashboards to log into — just a clear picture of the leads your website brought in.

10

Hosting + SSL

Fast, secure hosting with SSL is included on Monthly at no extra cost, so your site stays live, quick, and protected. On Lump Sum, hosting and SSL are available for $25/mo, billed separately.

11

Google Business Profile Support

Your Google Business Profile is often the first impression a homeowner gets before they ever visit your website. We keep it complete, accurate, and active — so it works as hard as your site to drive calls and quote requests.

12

Website Updates

Monthly includes unlimited edits — send a text or email and we handle it fast. No support tickets, no delays — direct access to the person who built your site. Lump Sum is a one-time build; updates after launch are handled separately.
