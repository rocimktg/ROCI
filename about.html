About Us

The Story

Websites Built With Purpose & Intent

I'm JJ Jaggers, owner and developer at Rocinante Marketing. I spent a decade at Federal Heath, a national sign manufacturer, working my way from Art Director to Digital Marketing Manager. During that time, I rebuilt the company's digital footprint across five divisions, owned advertising and lead generation strategy, and learned firsthand how visibility, clarity, and execution drive results at scale.

What I noticed along the way was that many local businesses rarely had access to that level of attention. Small teams, service companies, neighborhood brands, and independent operators were often stuck with template sites, slow load times, unclear messaging, outdated photos, or no one to call when something needed to change.

Rocinante Marketing helps local businesses look professional online, earn trust, and turn visitors into real leads. We work with a wide range of small businesses that depend on clear messaging, strong first impressions, and easy next steps.

We build fast, hand-coded websites designed to perform, not just sit there. Every site is built around speed, clarity, mobile usability, and the actions that matter most: calls, quote requests, appointments, and messages.

We handle your online presence like a dedicated digital partner, keeping your site updated, helping improve your local visibility, and making sure everything runs smoothly.
