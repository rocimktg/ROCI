Websites Built With Purpose & Intent

--- HEADER ---
FAQ
Questions & Answers
Straight Answers.
Everything contractors ask before signing on.

--- FAQ ITEMS ---
How do your website plans work and what is the difference?
Two plans. No setup fees.

The Monthly Plan ($175/mo): Your site, fully managed. We build it, host it, keep it updated, and help your reputation grow. Includes unlimited edits, a custom Reputation Page, and a monthly insights report. 12-month agreement required.

The Lump Sum ($3,500 one-time): A custom site built right and handed off to you. You own it completely. Host it yourself or add hosting ($25/mo) and unlimited edits ($50/mo) as needed.

Do you build multi-page websites?
Yes. We build both single-page and multi-page sites — the structure and pages are entirely up to you.

For most contractors, a single-page site is the right call. Everything a visitor needs — services, reviews, photos, contact — is right there, fast and focused. It converts well on mobile and gets your phone ringing.

If you'd rather have dedicated pages for whatever sections matter to your business, we build that too. Same hand-coded quality, same performance standards. We'll talk through what you want on the first call and build accordingly.

Do I own my website?
Short answer — it depends on how you want to work together.

Monthly: Your content is fully portable — domain, logo, photography, and written copy are yours permanently. The website code and hosting environment are owned by Rocinante Marketing. If you cancel after your 12-month term, we transfer your domain and deliver all your business content within 5 business days.

Lump Sum: You own the code and all files outright. We deliver everything you need to host it anywhere — your own hosting account, a provider of your choice, wherever you want. The site is yours to take and run independently.

What happens if I cancel my monthly plan?
The Monthly Plan starts with a 12-month commitment — after that, it's month-to-month with 30 days' notice. If you cancel, the site comes down since it runs on our infrastructure. But your domain, photos, and copy are always yours to take with you.

How does billing work?
No Setup Fees: Your first monthly payment is due at project kickoff to start the build.
Automatic Billing: Recurring payments are handled automatically via Stripe on the same day each month.
Price Lock: Your monthly rate is locked in for your initial 12-month term.

What do you need from me to get started?
Not much. Once you're ready to move forward, we'll need your logo, a few photos of your work, and basic business info — services, service area, contact details. If you don't have professional photos yet, we'll work with what you have. We handle everything else from there.

Can you write the copy for my site?
Yes. You don't need to write a word. We'll pull what we need from our kickoff call — your services, your market, what makes you different — and write copy that sounds like a real contractor, not a template.

What happens if I need changes after launch on the Lump Sum plan?
The Lump Sum is a one-time build — edits aren't included by default. If you need ongoing updates, you can add our edits package for $50/mo, which covers unlimited changes. Note that the edits package requires hosting through Rocinante Marketing ($25/mo). Or if you'd rather have the full managed experience, the Monthly plan at $175/mo includes everything — edits, hosting, GMB management, and more.

How fast will my site launch?
Once we have your payment and materials — logo, business info, photos — we get to work. Most sites are live within 7–10 business days.

Can I add more than 5 pages?
Yes. Additional pages are $100 each, one-time, regardless of which plan you're on. No package changes required — just let us know what you need.

Why don't you use WordPress or page builders?
WordPress sites rely on plugins that break and create security risks. Page builders bloat your code and slow your site down. We hand-code everything from scratch. The result is a site that loads in under a second, never breaks on its own, and has nothing for hackers to exploit.

What is the Reputation Page?
The Reputation Page is a simple feedback page built into every site we build — accessible at your domain (e.g., yourbusiness.com/feedback). When a job is done, you send the customer a link. They land on a clean, mobile-friendly page where they can leave feedback directly. It gives customers an easy way to share their experience, and gives you a private channel to catch any issues before they go public. Included in both plans. See Demo.

Do you manage Google Business profiles?
Yes, and it's included in the Monthly plan. We start with a full audit to make sure your profile is complete and accurate, then keep it active — adding photos and updates from jobs you send us via text or email. A complete, active profile ranks higher in local search and turns more map views into calls.
