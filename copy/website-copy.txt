Questions & Answers
FAQ for Rocinante Marketing
Get clear answers to pricing, timelines, ownership, hosting, and support so you can decide if we're the right fit.

--- FAQ ITEMS ---

Q: How do your website plans work?
A: We offer two straightforward plans built for home service contractors. No setup fees — just a professional site that's ready to work from day one.

The Foundation Plan ($125/mo): Built to get you professional and online, fast. This is for contractors who need a high-impact digital presence that looks great on a phone, captures calls and form fills, and tells customers you're the real deal. We handle the hosting, keep the site secure, and make sure your leads can actually call you and forms land in your inbox. Foundation launches as a finished, stable site — critical info like your phone number or hours can always be corrected at no charge, but for ongoing content updates, Pro is the right fit. 12-month agreement required.

The Pro Plan ($175/mo): Everything in Foundation, plus unlimited updates with 24-hour turnaround, direct access to the owner's cell so you can text photos straight from the job site, our custom Reputation Page to help you turn every finished job into a 5-star Google review, and a monthly insights report so you always know how your site is performing. 12-month agreement required.

Q: Which plan is right for me?
A: Both plans give you a fast, professional website with no setup fees and predictable flat-rate billing. Foundation is the right call if you need to get online quickly and keep overhead low — it launches as a finished site that captures leads around the clock and runs without ongoing changes. Pro is the better fit if you're actively growing and want a faster, more personal line of support — unlimited edits, direct cell access, and monthly reporting included.

Q: What's the difference between Foundation and Pro?
A: Foundation is your permanent digital foundation — built right, launched, and left to run. Critical business info corrections are always covered. The biggest difference is your level of access and how fast things move. Pro gives you direct access to the owner's cell number so you can text, send photos from the job site, and get content updates turned around within 24 hours. You also get the Reputation Page, a monthly insights report, and instant lead notifications. If you're actively growing and want a faster, more personal line of communication, Pro is the better fit.

Q: Can I upgrade from Foundation to Pro later?
A: Yes, absolutely. You can upgrade at any time — no waiting period, no new agreement required. We'll add your Reputation Page, bump you to unlimited edits with 24-hour turnaround, and switch you to direct cell access the same day. Just reach out and we'll handle the rest.

Q: Does Foundation include content updates?
A: Foundation is a set-it-and-forget-it plan. During the build, we work together until everything is right — then it launches as a polished, permanent asset. If your core business info ever changes (phone number, hours, address), we'll update it at no charge. Content updates beyond that — new services, photos, copy changes — are what Pro is built for.

Q: What is the Monthly Insights Report?
A: Every month we send you a clean report showing how your site is performing — traffic, where visitors are coming from, what pages they're looking at, and how many leads came in. You stay informed without having to log in anywhere. Included with every Pro plan.

Q: What kind of website do you build?
A: Every site we build is a single-page, hand-coded static website — no WordPress, no templates, no page builders. Single-page doesn't mean simple. It means everything a visitor needs to call you, trust you, and book you is on one fast-loading, mobile-first page. No clicking around, no getting lost.

Q: Why a single-page site instead of multiple pages?
A: A single-page site showcases your services, reviews, photos, and contact info instantly. It loads faster, converts better on mobile, and is easier to maintain — a deliberate choice, not a shortcut.

Q: Why don't you use WordPress or page builders?
A: WordPress sites rely on plugins that break, require constant updates, and create security vulnerabilities. Page builders bloat your code and slow your site down. We hand-code everything from scratch — the result is a site that loads in under a second, never breaks on its own, and has nothing for hackers to exploit.

Q: What is the Reputation Page? [Pro Feature]
A: The Reputation Page is a simple feedback page built into every Pro site — accessible at your domain (e.g., yourbusiness.com/feedback). When a job is done, you send the customer a link. They land on a clean, mobile-friendly page where they can tap straight to Google, Nextdoor, HomeAdvisor, Thumbtack, or Yelp to leave a review. Included in the Pro Plan at no extra charge.

Q: What do I need to provide to get started?
A: Very little — that's the point. We do the heavy lifting upfront. We'll need your basic business info (services, hours, contact details), any existing brand assets like a logo, and access to your domain. If you don't have a domain we can help you get one. Most clients are surprised by how little we ask for.

Q: Do I own my domain and content?
A: Yes, always. Your domain, photography, and written content are yours permanently. If we purchase a domain on your behalf we transfer it to you upon request. You are never held hostage.

Q: What happens if I cancel my monthly plan?
A: Your domain, photos, logo, and written content are always yours. The website itself runs on our infrastructure — if the subscription ends the site comes down, but everything that belongs to your brand comes with you. After your 12-month agreement your plan rolls month-to-month with no action required on your part.

Q: How does billing work?
A: Your first payment is charged at project kickoff. Recurring billing begins automatically one month later through Stripe.
