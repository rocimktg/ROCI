Questions & Answers
FAQ for Rocinante Marketing
Get clear answers to pricing, timelines, ownership, hosting, and support so you can decide if we're the right fit.

--- FAQ ITEMS ---
Q: How do your website plans work and what is the difference?
A: We offer two straightforward plans built for home service contractors. No setup fees. Both plans include a direct line to the owner via text, email, or phone, and both offer a 2-business day turnaround on requests.

The Foundation Plan ($125/mo): Built to get you professional and online, fast. This plan includes email lead notifications and covers corrections to your critical business info (phone, hours, address). It's perfect for a low-maintenance, high-impact digital presence. 12-month agreement required.

The Pro Plan ($175/mo): Everything in Foundation, plus unlimited content updates (send us new job site photos or text changes anytime), our custom Reputation Page to boost reviews, monthly insights reports, SMS lead notifications, and covers corrections to your critical business info (phone, hours, address) within 2 business days. 12-month agreement required.

Q: Can I upgrade from Foundation to Pro later?
A: Yes, absolutely. You can upgrade at any time. We'll add your Reputation Page, switch you to unlimited content updates, and start your monthly reporting immediately. Your existing 12-month commitment remains in place.

Q: Do I own my website?
A: We operate on a service-based rental model. You own all your content. Your domain, logo, photography, and written copy are yours permanently. The website itself (the custom code and hosting environment) is owned by Rocinante Marketing. If you choose to cancel after your 12-month term, we provide you with all your business content and transfer your domain to you within 5 business days.

Q: What happens if I cancel my monthly plan?
A: This agreement carries a 12-month minimum commitment. After that, your plan rolls month-to-month and can be canceled with 30 days' notice. Because the site runs on our proprietary infrastructure, the website goes offline if the subscription ends. However, your domain, photos, and text are always yours to take with you.

Q: How does billing work?
A: No Setup Fees: Your first monthly payment is due at project kickoff to start the build.

Automatic Billing: Recurring payments are handled automatically via Stripe on the same day each month.

Price Lock: Your monthly rate is locked in for your initial 12-month term.

Q: How fast will my site launch?
A: Once we receive your initial payment and materials (logo, business info, etc.), we move fast. Our target launch timeline is within 10 business days.

Q: Why a single-page site instead of multiple pages?
A: A single-page site showcases your services, reviews, photos, and contact info instantly. It loads faster, converts better on mobile, and is easier to maintain, a deliberate choice to get your phone ringing, not a shortcut.

Q: Why don't you use WordPress or page builders?
A: WordPress sites rely on plugins that break and create security risks. Page builders bloat your code and slow your site down. We hand-code everything from scratch. The result is a site that loads in under a second, never breaks on its own, and has nothing for hackers to exploit.

Q: What are Instant Lead Notifications? [Pro Feature]
A: Every site we build delivers leads to your email address the second a customer hits "submit," regardless of your plan. For Pro Plan subscribers, we also set up SMS notifications. This sends a text message directly to your cell phone so you can follow up with potential customers immediately, even if you're away from your computer or out on a job site.

Q: What is the Reputation Page? [Pro Feature]
A: The Reputation Page is a simple feedback page built into every site we build — accessible at your domain (e.g., yourbusiness.com/feedback). When a job is done, you send the customer a link. They land on a clean, mobile-friendly page where they can leave feedback directly. It gives customers an easy way to share their experience, and gives you a private channel to hear from anyone who wasn't fully satisfied before it becomes a public issue. Included in the Pro Plan at no extra charge. See Demo.
