FAQ
Questions & Answers
FAQ for Rocinante Marketing
Get clear answers to pricing, timelines, ownership, hosting, and support so you can decide if we're the right fit.

---

How do your website plans work?

We offer three no-nonsense ways to get your business in front of local customers. No setup fees — just a professional site that's ready to work today.

The Starter Plan ($125/mo): Built to get you professional and online, fast. This is for the contractors who need a high-impact "digital sign" that looks great on a phone and tells customers you're the real deal. We handle the hosting, keep the site secure, and make sure your leads can actually call you and forms land in your inbox. Unlimited email support is included — perfect if you want to set it, forget it, and get back to work. 12-month agreement required.

The Pro Plan ($175/mo): A rapid-launch powerhouse that does the heavy lifting for you. This includes everything in the Starter Plan, plus unlimited updates with 24-hour turnaround, direct access to the owner's cell so you can text photos straight from the job site, and our custom Reputation Page to help you turn every finished job into a 5-star Google review. You also get Google Analytics setup, a monthly analytics report delivered to your inbox, and a quarterly check-in call to make sure everything is working for your business. 12-month agreement required.

The Lump Sum ($2,800): A one-time investment for established brands who want to own their digital asset outright. You buy the site today and never worry about a monthly subscription again. You own the code and the work forever. If you still want us to handle the technical stuff, hosting ($25/mo) and unlimited updates ($50/mo) are available as simple add-ons.

---

Which plan is right for me?

Most contractors go monthly. You get everything included for a predictable flat rate, no large upfront cost, and direct support for as long as you're a client. It's the better fit for businesses that want to stay lean and keep overhead low.

The lump sum makes sense if you prefer owning your assets outright and don't mind a larger upfront investment. Over time the monthly cost is lower, but you manage hosting and edits separately.

---

What's the difference between Starter and Pro?

The biggest difference is your level of access and how fast things move. Starter gives you unlimited email support with a 72-hour turnaround — great if you're hands-off and just need the site maintained. Pro gives you direct access to the owner's cell number so you can text, send photos from the job site, and get updates turned around within 24 hours. You also get the Reputation Page, Google Analytics setup, a monthly analytics report delivered to your inbox, and a quarterly check-in call to review performance and make sure everything is working for your business. If you're actively growing and want a faster, more personal line of communication, Pro is the better fit.

---

Can I upgrade from Starter to Pro later?

Yes, absolutely. You can upgrade at any time — no waiting period, no new agreement required. We'll add your Reputation Page, bump you to unlimited edits with 24-hour turnaround, and switch you to direct cell access the same day. Just reach out and we'll handle the rest.

---

Does Starter include content updates?

Yes. Starter includes unlimited content edit requests via email, with a 72-hour turnaround on standard changes like text updates, image swaps, and hours. If you need faster turnaround and a more direct line, Pro includes 24-hour priority support with direct access to the owner's cell — text, photos from the job site, whatever you need.

---

What is Google Analytics and why does it matter?

Google Analytics is a free tool that shows you exactly how your website is performing — how many people visited, where they came from, what they looked at, and how long they stayed. For contractors, it means you can see whether your site is actually generating traffic, which services people are searching for, and whether your marketing is working. We set it up, connect it to your site so the data is flowing from day one, and send you a clean monthly report every month so you always know how your site is performing. Included with every Pro plan.

---

What kind of website do you build?

Every site we build is a single-page, hand-coded static website — no WordPress, no templates, no page builders. Single-page doesn't mean simple. It means everything a visitor needs to call you, trust you, and book you is on one fast-loading, mobile-first page. No clicking around, no getting lost.

---

Why a single-page site instead of multiple pages?

A single-page site showcases your services, reviews, photos, and contact info instantly. It loads faster, converts better on mobile, and is easier to maintain — a deliberate choice, not a shortcut.

---

Why don't you use WordPress or page builders?

WordPress sites rely on plugins that break, require constant updates, and create security vulnerabilities. Page builders bloat your code and slow your site down. We hand-code everything from scratch — the result is a site that loads in under a second, never breaks on its own, and has nothing for hackers to exploit.

---

What is the Reputation Page? — Pro Feature

The Reputation Page is a simple feedback page built into every site we build — accessible at your domain (e.g., yourbusiness.com/feedback). When a job is done, you send the customer a link. They land on a clean, mobile-friendly page where they can leave feedback directly. It gives customers an easy way to share their experience, and gives you a private channel to hear from anyone who wasn't fully satisfied before it becomes a public issue. Included in the Pro Plan at no extra charge.

---

What do I need to provide to get started?

Very little — that's the point. We do the heavy lifting upfront. We'll need your basic business info (services, hours, contact details), any existing brand assets like a logo, and access to your domain. If you don't have a domain we can help you get one. Most clients are surprised by how little we ask for.

---

How do I request changes after the site is live?

Just text or email directly. Standard content edits — text updates, image swaps, hours changes — turn around within 48 business hours. You never touch the code. Starter clients have unlimited email content edit requests with a 72-hour turnaround. Pro clients get direct access to the owner's cell with 24-hour priority turnaround. Lump sum clients can add the $50/mo unlimited edits add-on or request edits individually.

---

Do I own my domain and content?

Yes, always. Your domain, photography, and written content are yours permanently. If we purchase a domain on your behalf we transfer it to you upon request. You are never held hostage.

---

What happens if I cancel my monthly plan?

Your domain, photos, logo, and written content are always yours. The website itself runs on our infrastructure — if the subscription ends the site comes down, but everything that belongs to your brand comes with you. After your 12-month agreement your plan rolls month-to-month with no action required on your part.

---

How does billing work?

Monthly: Your first payment is charged at project kickoff. Recurring billing begins automatically one month later through Stripe.

Lump Sum: The full $2,800 is due before work begins, followed by the optional $25/mo hosting once the site is live.
