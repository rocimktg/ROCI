FAQ for Rocinante Marketing

Get clear answers to pricing, timelines, ownership, hosting, and support so you can decide if we're the right fit.

How do your website plans work?

We offer three distinct paths: Starter, Pro, and Lump Sum.

Starter and Pro are monthly subscription plans that include the build, hosting, unlimited content edits, and direct support. These require a 12-month agreement, after which they roll month-to-month.

Lump Sum is a one-time investment ($3,800) for those who want to own the asset outright with lower recurring monthly costs.

Which plan is right for me?

Starter ($150/mo): Best for businesses needing a high-performance, single-page "powerhouse" to capture leads without the complexity of a massive site.

Pro ($175/mo): Our most popular choice. It's a multi-page (up to 5) solution that includes on-page SEO and Google Analytics setup to help you rank higher in local searches.

Lump Sum ($3,800): Ideal for established brands that want a premium, 5-page site as a permanent asset. You pay once and enjoy a heavily reduced hosting fee of only $25/mo.

What happens if I cancel? Do I keep the website?

You always keep your domain, logo, photos, and copy.

For Subscription Plans (Starter/Pro): The website runs on our proprietary custom-coded infrastructure. If the subscription ends, the site is taken down, but you retain all your brand assets.

For Lump Sum: You own the site files. While we provide hosting for $25/mo, the site is yours to keep and move if you ever choose to leave.

Why don't you use WordPress or page builders?

We build custom-coded static sites. They stay fast, secure, and stable. Unlike WordPress, they load instantly, don't break when plugins update, and have no login pages for hackers to exploit. You get a performance-first build instead of a fragile patchwork of plugins.

How long does it take to build my site?

Most sites launch in 2–4 weeks. The timeline depends on how quickly you provide your business details and feedback. Once we have the necessary info, the build moves fast.

What do I need to provide before we start?

To get moving, we'll need:

  • Your business basics (services, hours, contact info).
  • Any existing brand assets (logo, high-res photos).
  • Key talking points or specific "must-have" text.
  • Domain credentials (or we can purchase a domain on your behalf).
How do I request updates or changes after the site is live?

Just text or email us. Standard content edits — text changes, image swaps, or updating hours — typically turn around within 48 business hours. Edits are included in the Starter and Pro plans (and available as an add-on for Lump Sum), so you never have to touch the "back end."

Do I own my domain and branding?

Yes. Your domain, photography, and written content are yours permanently. Even if we purchase the domain on your behalf, we transfer it to you if you ever cancel.

How do payments work?

Subscription: Your first monthly payment is due before work begins. Billing continues automatically through Stripe on the same calendar day.

Lump Sum: The one-time fee is paid upfront, followed by the $25/mo hosting fee once the site is live.

What happens after my 12-month agreement ends? (Starter/Pro)

Your plan rolls to month-to-month automatically. No action is needed on your part. We keep your site running, updated, and supported just like Day 1.

What happens if something happens to you?

Every site we build is version-controlled, backed up, and fully documented so another developer can step in smoothly if needed. Your domain and all brand assets are always yours, so you're never dependent on us to keep your business running.
