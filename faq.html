Straight Answers.

Everything local businesses ask before signing on.

What plans do you offer?

We offer three website plans with no setup fees.

Lite ($150/mo, month-to-month — no contract): A 5-page hand-coded site with hosting, SSL, tap-to-call, local search structure, a Review Request Page, and a one-time Google Business Profile setup and audit. No ownership option.

Growth ($350/mo, 12-month term): 5 core pages plus 3 service or city pages, monthly Google Business Profile management, call tracking, missed-call textback, and up to 1 new or improved page per month. Continues month-to-month after the initial term. Site ownership available after 12 months.

Pro ($750/mo, 12-month term): 5 core pages plus 10 service or city pages, weekly Google Business Profile management, call tracking, missed-call textback, up to 3 Google Search campaigns, and LSA setup and maintenance where available. Up to 2 new or improved pages per month. Ad spend is separate and paid directly to Google.

All plans include hosting, SSL, and a $0 setup fee.

How many pages do I get?

Lite includes 5 pages — enough to cover your services, service area, about, contact, and a homepage built to convert.

Growth includes 5 core pages plus 3 service or city pages, then up to 1 new or improved page per month.

Pro includes 5 core pages plus 10 service or city pages, then up to 2 new or improved pages per month.

Additional pages are $100 each, one-time, on any plan.

Ongoing page work can be a new service page, a new city page, or an improvement to an existing page. Unused page work does not roll over.

How does billing work?

There are no setup fees on any plan.

Your first monthly payment is due at kickoff to start the build. Lite is month-to-month with no contract. Growth and Pro both have a 12-month term, then continue month-to-month.

Site ownership is available on Growth and Pro after the 12-month term. Lite does not include a site ownership option.

Recurring payments are handled automatically through Stripe on the same billing date each month.

What do I own on my website plan?

Your domain, logo, photos, and written copy are always yours on any plan.

The live website runs on Rocinante Marketing's managed hosting while your monthly plan is active. Growth and Pro clients own the finished site after the 12-month term — most simply continue at the same monthly rate with hosting, updates, and support staying in place. Lite is month-to-month with no contract and does not include a site ownership option.

Can I buy out my website?

Yes, on Growth and Pro after 12 months. The buyout is $0 — you choose the buyout option and the finished website files are transferred to you.

From there, you can host the site wherever you want, or keep it hosted with Rocinante Marketing for $25/month.

Lite is month-to-month with no contract, but does not include a site ownership option.

What happens after my initial term?

On Growth and Pro, completing the 12-month term means the site is yours. The plan continues month-to-month at the same rate — your site stays live, edits continue, and all included services continue. If you want to stop, you take the files with you.

Lite has no initial term. It's month-to-month from the start, so there's nothing that changes — you can cancel anytime.

What happens if I cancel?

You can cancel before your next billing date.

If you cancel and do not choose buyout, the managed website comes offline at the end of your billing period.

No website files are transferred without buyout. Lite does not include a buyout option.

Your domain, logo, photos, and written copy remain yours.

What is the Review Request Page?

Every plan includes a Review Request Page at your domain, such as yourbusiness.com/feedback.

You send customers the link or a QR code after the job.

Happy customers can leave a public review on Google, Yelp, Nextdoor, or other platforms.

If something was off, they can contact you privately first. See Demo.

What do you need from me to get started?

Not much. Once you're ready to move forward, we'll need your logo, a few photos of your business, and basic business info — services, locations, contact details. If you don't have professional photos yet, we'll work with what you have. We handle everything else from there.

Can you write the copy for my site?

Yes. You don't need to write a word. We'll pull what we need from our kickoff call — your services, your market, what makes you different — and write copy that sounds like a real business, not a template.

How fast will my site launch?

Once we have your payment and materials — logo, business info, photos — we get to work. Most sites are live within 7–10 business days.

Do I have to commit before seeing the work?

No. Before you pay anything, you'll see a working homepage built around your business — your services, your market, your name on it. Not a mockup or a template with your logo swapped in. A real, hand-coded page.

If it doesn't look right or feel like a fit, you walk away with nothing owed. Most people are ready to move forward after seeing it.

Why don't you use WordPress or page builders?

WordPress sites rely on plugins that break and create security risks. Page builders bloat your code and slow your site down. We hand-code everything from scratch. The result is a site that loads in under a second, never breaks on its own, and has nothing for hackers to exploit.

Do you manage Google Business Profiles?

Yes. Google Business Profile support is built into all three plans.

Lite includes a one-time setup and audit to make sure your profile is complete and aligned with your website.

Growth includes monthly Google Business Profile management — keeping your listing active, accurate, and updated with content from jobs you send us.

Pro includes active weekly Google Business Profile management plus up to 3 Google Search campaigns and Local Services Ads setup and maintenance where available. Ad spend is paid directly to Google and is separate from the plan.

Is ad spend included?

No. Google Ads and Local Services Ads spend is paid directly to Google and is not included in the monthly plan.

Pro includes the setup, management, and ongoing maintenance for Search campaigns and LSA — not the underlying media spend. Lite and Growth do not include paid ad management.

Can every business run Local Services Ads?

Not always. Local Services Ads availability, approval requirements, and lead volume vary by location and business category.

Pro includes setup/support where LSA is available and makes sense.

What does ongoing page work mean?

It means we can add or improve service, location, or specialty pages over time based on what people are searching for and what your business wants to target.

Some months that may mean a new page. Other months it may mean improving an existing page.

Growth includes up to one per month. Pro includes up to two per month.

Unused ongoing page work does not roll over.

What do website updates include?

Growth and Pro include unlimited website updates — reasonable text, photo, service, and contact changes to existing pages. New pages, new major sections, custom features, and full redesigns are not included unless specified in the plan.

Lite includes an annual content refresh plus operational edits — things like phone number changes, photo swaps, service updates, and minor text changes. New pages, new sections, or larger rebuilds on Lite are quoted separately or available as add-ons.

Do local businesses really need a website?

Yes. Local business decisions are built on trust, clarity, and convenience. People compare companies quickly, especially on mobile. A fast, professional website helps you look credible, explain what you offer, and make it easy to call.

How do I rank my business on Google Maps?

Google Maps ranking comes down to three things: a complete and active Google Business Profile, a fast website, and consistent five-star reviews.

Your Google Business Profile needs accurate business categories, real photos, and regular updates. Your website needs to load fast, mention your city and services clearly, and link to your Google Business Profile. Reviews are the multiplier — the more recent and relevant, the higher you climb.

Lite includes a one-time profile setup and audit. Growth includes monthly Google Business Profile management. Pro includes active weekly management — we keep your profile current with photos and posts from jobs you send us.

What does the Lite plan include?

Lite is a 5-page hand-coded website built for businesses that need a professional online presence without a full local SEO buildout. It covers the essentials — homepage, services, about, service area, and contact — with tap-to-call, local search structure, and a Review Request Page included.

It also includes a one-time Google Business Profile setup and audit, hosting, SSL, and an annual content refresh plus operational edits. If you want dedicated location pages, ongoing page expansion, and monthly Google Profile management, Growth is the better fit.

Which plan is right for my business?

Lite is best for businesses that need a professional website and a solid Google Profile foundation without a long-term commitment.

Growth is best for businesses focused on local search — you want service and city pages, monthly Google Profile management, call tracking, and ongoing page expansion.

Pro is best for businesses ready to run paid ads alongside their organic presence — Google Search campaigns, LSA support, weekly Google Profile management, and broader location coverage.
