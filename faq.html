FAQ for Rocinante Marketing

Get clear answers to pricing, timelines, ownership, hosting, and support so you can decide if we're the right fit.

How does your monthly website plan work?

We offer three plans — Starter, Standard, and Pro — starting at $125/mo. Each plan includes a custom hand-coded website, hosting, unlimited edits, and direct owner support. Higher tiers add more pages, on-page SEO, and Google Business Profile management. All plans require a 12-month agreement, after which they roll month-to-month.

Which plan is right for me?

Starter is a great fit if you need a clean, professional one-page site to establish an online presence. Standard works well for growing businesses that need multiple pages to showcase different services. Pro is designed for contractors who want hands-off management — it includes full Google Business Profile management on top of the standard website features.

What does Google Business Profile management include?

Available on the Pro plan, we actively manage your Google Business Profile on your behalf each month. That includes responding to customer reviews, uploading fresh job site photos, keeping your business info accurate, and making sure your profile stays optimized for local search. For contractors, your Google listing is often the first thing a potential customer sees — we make sure it's working for you.

What happens if I cancel? Do I keep the website?

You keep your domain, logo, photos, copy, and every other brand asset. The website itself runs on our hosting infrastructure, so if the subscription ends the site comes down — but you're never left without the content and branding that represent your business.

How long does it take to build my site?

Most sites launch in 2–4 weeks. The timeline depends on how quickly you provide content and feedback, and how many pages we're building. Once we have what we need, the build moves fast.

What do I need to provide before we start?

To get moving we'll need:

  • Your business basics (services, hours, contact info)
  • Any existing brand assets
  • Must-have copy or talking points
  • Domain credentials, or we can purchase one on your behalf
How do I request updates or changes after the site is live?

Just text or email with what you need. Standard content edits — text changes, image swaps, hours updates — turn around within 48 business hours. Edits are included in every plan, so there are no surprise invoices and you never have to touch the back end.

Why don't you use WordPress or page builders?

Custom-coded static sites stay fast, secure, and stable. They load instantly, don't break when plugins update, present no login page for hackers, and cost less to maintain. You get a performance-first build instead of a fragile patchwork of plugins.

Do I own my domain and branding?

Yes. Your domain, photography, and written content are yours permanently. Even if we purchase the domain on your behalf, we transfer it to you if you ever cancel.

How do payments work?

Your first monthly payment is due before work begins. After that, billing continues automatically each month through Stripe on the same calendar day. No setup fees, no surprises.

What happens after my 12-month agreement ends?

After your initial 12-month agreement your plan rolls month-to-month automatically — no action needed on your part. Pricing may be adjusted annually by no more than 5%, and you'll always receive 30 days written notice before any change takes effect.

What happens if something happens to you?

Every site we build is version-controlled, backed up, and fully documented so another developer can step in smoothly if needed. Your domain and all brand assets are always yours, so you're never dependent on us to keep your business running.
