FAQ for Rocinante Marketing

Get clear answers to pricing, timelines, ownership, hosting, and support so you can decide if we're the right fit.

How do your website plans work?

We offer two straightforward options.

The Monthly Plan ($200/mo) includes everything — the build, hosting, forms, Google Analytics, the Reputation Page, and unlimited content edits. There's no setup fee. Your first payment is due at kickoff, and billing continues automatically one month later. A 12-month agreement is required, after which it rolls month-to-month.

The Lump Sum ($2,800) is a one-time investment for contractors who want to own the asset outright. Hosting is available for $25/mo and unlimited content edits for $50/mo as optional add-ons.

Which plan is right for me?

Most contractors go monthly. You get everything included for a predictable flat rate, no large upfront cost, and direct support for as long as you're a client. It's the better fit for businesses that want to stay lean and keep overhead low.

The lump sum makes sense if you prefer owning your assets outright and don't mind a larger upfront investment. Over time the monthly cost is lower, but you manage hosting and edits separately.

What kind of website do you build?

Every site we build is a single-page, hand-coded static website — no WordPress, no templates, no page builders. Single-page doesn't mean simple. It means everything a visitor needs to call you, trust you, and book you is on one fast-loading, mobile-first page. No clicking around, no getting lost.

Why a single-page site instead of multiple pages?

Because contractors don't need a blog, a careers page, and a photo gallery — they need someone to call them. A single-page site puts your services, reviews, photos, and contact information directly in front of the visitor with no friction. It loads faster, converts better on mobile, and is easier to maintain. It's a purposeful decision, not a shortcut.

Why don't you use WordPress or page builders?

WordPress sites rely on plugins that break, require constant updates, and create security vulnerabilities. Page builders bloat your code and slow your site down. We hand-code everything from scratch — the result is a site that loads in under a second, never breaks on its own, and has nothing for hackers to exploit.

What is the Reputation Page?

The Reputation Page is a simple feedback page built into every site we build — accessible at your domain (e.g., yourbusiness.com/feedback). When a job is done, you send the customer a link. They land on a clean, mobile-friendly page where they can leave feedback directly. It gives customers an easy way to share their experience, and gives you a private channel to hear from anyone who wasn't fully satisfied before it becomes a public issue. Included in every monthly plan at no extra charge.

What do I need to provide to get started?

Very little — that's the point. We do the heavy lifting upfront. We'll need your basic business info (services, hours, contact details), any existing brand assets like a logo, and access to your domain. If you don't have a domain we can help you get one. Most clients are surprised by how little we ask for.

How do I request changes after the site is live?

Just text or email directly. Standard content edits — text updates, image swaps, hours changes — turn around within 48 business hours. You never touch the code. Monthly plan clients have unlimited content edits included. Lump sum clients can add the $50/mo unlimited edits add-on or request edits individually.

Do I own my domain and content?

Yes, always. Your domain, photography, and written content are yours permanently. If we purchase a domain on your behalf we transfer it to you upon request. You are never held hostage.

What happens if I cancel my monthly plan?

Your domain, photos, logo, and written content are always yours. The website itself runs on our infrastructure — if the subscription ends the site comes down, but everything that belongs to your brand comes with you. After your 12-month agreement your plan rolls month-to-month with no action required on your part.

How does billing work?

Monthly: Your first payment is charged at project kickoff. Recurring billing begins automatically one month later through Stripe.

Lump Sum: The full $2,800 is due before work begins, followed by the optional $25/mo hosting once the site is live.
