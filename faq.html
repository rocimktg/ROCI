Straight Answers.

Everything contractors ask before signing on.

How do your website plans work and what is the difference?

Two plans. No setup fees.

The Monthly Plan ($175/mo): Your site, fully managed. We build it, host it, keep it updated, and help your reputation grow. Includes unlimited edits, a custom Reputation Page, and a monthly insights report. 12-month agreement required.

The Lump Sum ($3,500 one-time): A custom site built right and handed off to you. You own it completely. Host it yourself or add hosting ($25/mo) and unlimited edits ($50/mo) as needed.

Do I own my website?

Short answer — it depends on how you want to work together.

Monthly: We operate on a service-based model. You own all your content — domain, logo, photography, and written copy — permanently. The website code and hosting environment are owned by Rocinante Marketing. If you cancel after your 12-month term, we provide all your business content and transfer your domain within 5 business days.

Lump Sum: You own everything — the code, the site, the hosting environment. It's yours outright.

What happens if I cancel my monthly plan?

The Monthly Plan starts with a 12-month commitment — after that, it's month-to-month with 30 days' notice. If you cancel, the site comes down since it runs on our infrastructure. But your domain, photos, and copy are always yours to take with you.

How does billing work?

No Setup Fees: Your first monthly payment is due at project kickoff to start the build.

Automatic Billing: Recurring payments are handled automatically via Stripe on the same day each month.

Price Lock: Your monthly rate is locked in for your initial 12-month term.

How fast will my site launch?

Once we have your payment and materials — logo, business info, photos — we get to work. Most sites are live within 7–10 business days.

Why a single-page site instead of multiple pages?

A single-page site showcases your services, reviews, photos, and contact info instantly. It loads faster, converts better on mobile, and is easier to maintain — a deliberate choice to get your phone ringing, not a shortcut.

Why don't you use WordPress or page builders?

WordPress sites rely on plugins that break and create security risks. Page builders bloat your code and slow your site down. We hand-code everything from scratch. The result is a site that loads in under a second, never breaks on its own, and has nothing for hackers to exploit.

What is the Reputation Page?

The Reputation Page is a simple feedback page built into every site we build — accessible at your domain (e.g., yourbusiness.com/feedback). When a job is done, you send the customer a link. They land on a clean, mobile-friendly page where they can leave feedback directly. It gives customers an easy way to share their experience, and gives you a private channel to catch any issues before they go public. Included in both plans. See Demo.
